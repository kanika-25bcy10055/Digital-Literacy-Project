========================================
Task 4 – Social Media Do’s and Don’ts
========================================

-------------------------
✔ Do’s
-------------------------
1. Use professional and respectful language
2. Keep your profile clean and updated
3. Think before posting anything online
4. Respect others’ opinions and privacy
5. Protect your personal information

-------------------------
❌ Don’ts
-------------------------
1. Do not share sensitive or personal information
2. Do not use abusive or inappropriate language
3. Do not post offensive or harmful content
4. Do not spread fake news or misinformation
5. Do not overshare personal details online
